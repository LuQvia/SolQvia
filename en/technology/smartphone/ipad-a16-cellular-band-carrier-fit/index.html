English technical guide: iPad A16 Cellular Band Carrier Fit

This page is the English companion to the corresponding SolQvia Japan guide. It preserves the diagnostic boundary used by the Japanese article: distinguish device settings, carrier/account conditions, radio compatibility and service provisioning instead of treating them as one problem.

Recommended diagnostic order

  1. Identify the exact model number and sales region.
  2. Compare LTE and 5G bands with the target Japan carrier.
  3. Check physical SIM/eSIM layout and carrier provisioning requirements.
  4. Verify VoLTE/IMS, SMS and emergency-call support separately from bands.
  5. For imported devices, verify Japan regulatory/technical-conformity requirements and carrier support.

Important boundary

Do not treat a single successful check as proof of full compatibility. SIM recognition, mobile data, voice, SMS, eSIM provisioning, APN and 5G can succeed or fail independently. Preserve a known-good state before factory reset, eSIM deletion or account removal.

Answer first: compare the real display with a screenshot to separate rendering from panel hardware

Flicker and lines can come from display settings, an app or OS rendering issue, or physical display damage. A screenshot test plus low-impact software checks can help separate those paths.

Separate likely causes by symptom

SymptomCheckInterpretation
Issue appears in screenshotCheck app/OS/renderingSoftware path first
Real screen is bad, screenshot looks normalDisplay hardware is more suspectHardware path
Flicker changes with brightnessCheck brightness/adaptive display settingsPossible setting dependency
Only one app is affectedUpdate/restart the appApp-specific issue
Started after impact/pressure/liquidSeek repairPossible physical damage

Check one low-impact condition at a time

  1. 1
    Restart the phone
  2. 2
    Take a screenshot and compare the saved image with the physical display
  3. 3
    Check brightness, adaptive brightness, color/display, and power-saving settings
  4. 4
    If only one app is affected, update or restart it
  5. 5
    Check OS updates
  6. 6
    If lines or flashing persist after impact or liquid exposure, move to repair

Avoid changing several conditions at once; retest after each change so the cause remains identifiable.

When to move to repair or official support

  • The issue began after a drop, pressure, or liquid exposure
  • It persists after restart, updates, and accessory/environment checks
  • There is overheating, odor, port damage, battery swelling, or another safety concern

Treat erase/factory reset as a later option only after backups and account recovery paths are confirmed.

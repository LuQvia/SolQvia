Answer first: clean and isolate the screen before moving to restart, apps, and hardware

Touch problems can come from moisture or dirt, screen protectors and cases, a frozen app or OS, or physical damage. Start with low-impact checks and avoid a factory reset until simpler causes are excluded.

Separate likely causes by symptom

SymptomCheckInterpretation
Wet/dirty screenDry and clean the displaySurface condition may be the cause
Only bad with protector/caseTemporarily remove itIsolate thickness, lift, or edge interference
Only one app is affectedUpdate/close the app and retestApp-specific issue
Whole phone is affectedRestart and check OS updatesSystem-level issue
Started after drop/liquid exposureStop forcing input and seek official repairPossible hardware damage

Check one low-impact condition at a time

  1. 1
    Dry and clean the screen and your hands
  2. 2
    Temporarily remove the screen protector, case, and attached accessories
  3. 3
    Restart or use the model-specific forced restart procedure
  4. 4
    If only one app is affected, test after updating or restarting the app
  5. 5
    Check OS updates and free storage
  6. 6
    If the issue follows a drop, liquid exposure, or cracked display, move to repair

Avoid changing several conditions at once; retest after each change so the cause remains identifiable.

When to move to repair or official support

  • The issue began after a drop, pressure, or liquid exposure
  • It persists after restart, updates, and accessory/environment checks
  • There is overheating, odor, port damage, battery swelling, or another safety concern

Treat erase/factory reset as a later option only after backups and account recovery paths are confirmed.

Support life is not inferred from release year alone

A fixed rule such as “a phone is old after a certain number of years” can misrepresent manufacturer policies and exact-model support. This database separates current official OS and security-support signals from the sales records used elsewhere in SolQvia.

Explicitly documented support and fields that still require exact-model confirmation remain distinct. Inability to install the newest OS does not automatically mean the phone is unusable, while OS eligibility alone does not guarantee application support, repair-parts availability or continued carrier functionality.

For a used-device decision, combine this lifecycle check with the current repair route and the condition of the actual device, especially when the phone is expected to remain in service for several years.

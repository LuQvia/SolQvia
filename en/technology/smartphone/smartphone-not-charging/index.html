Answer first: test a known-good cable/charger, inspect the port, and rule out moisture or temperature protection

Charging failures can come from the cable, charger, outlet, port debris or moisture, temperature protection, charge-limit settings, battery condition, or the phone itself. Change one variable at a time.

Separate likely causes by symptom

SymptomCheckInterpretation
Works with another cableCable issueReplace cable
Works with another chargerCharger issueCheck output/failure
Debris/moisture warningPort conditionDo not force the connector
Stops when hot/coldTemperature protectionReturn to normal temperature
Stops near 80%Charge limit/optimizationMay be expected behavior
No response with multiple good chargersPort/battery/deviceRepair path

Check one low-impact condition at a time

  1. 1
    Try another known-good cable, charger, and outlet
  2. 2
    Inspect the port for debris, damage, or moisture warning
  3. 3
    Return the phone to a normal temperature
  4. 4
    Restart and check OS updates
  5. 5
    Check battery protection / charge-limit settings
  6. 6
    If multiple known-good combinations fail, move to repair

Avoid changing several conditions at once; retest after each change so the cause remains identifiable.

When to move to repair or official support

  • The issue began after a drop, pressure, or liquid exposure
  • It persists after restart, updates, and accessory/environment checks
  • There is overheating, odor, port damage, battery swelling, or another safety concern

Treat erase/factory reset as a later option only after backups and account recovery paths are confirmed.

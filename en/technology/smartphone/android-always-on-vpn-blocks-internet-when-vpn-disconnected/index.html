What “Block connections without VPN” means on Android — and why the internet stops when VPN disconnects

“Block connections without VPN” prevents traffic from using a non-VPN path. If the VPN drops, Wi-Fi or cellular may still appear connected while apps remain offline because Android is intentionally blocking traffic outside the VPN.

Answer first: the setting is designed to stop non-VPN traffic

Android separates Always-on VPN from Block connections without VPN. Always-on VPN tries to keep the VPN active. The blocking option prevents traffic from falling back to a normal Wi-Fi or cellular path when the VPN is unavailable.

StateExpected behavior
VPN connectedTraffic uses the VPN
VPN disconnected, blocking offNormal network traffic may continue
VPN disconnected, blocking onNon-VPN traffic is blocked

Where to check the setting

  1. Open Settings.
  2. Open Network & internet → VPN. If the menu differs, search Settings for “VPN.”
  3. Open the settings icon for the relevant VPN.
  4. Check Always-on VPN.
  5. Check Block connections without VPN if the option is present.

On a personally managed device, turning off the block can restore ordinary network access when the VPN is disconnected. That also means the traffic is no longer protected by the VPN, so do not disable it without understanding why it was enabled.

Do not confuse VPN lockdown with Private DNS or APN

SettingPurpose
Always-on VPNKeeps or restarts a VPN connection
Block connections without VPNBlocks traffic that is outside the VPN
Private DNSControls DNS resolution behavior
APNDefines cellular packet-data access settings

If both Wi-Fi and cellular stop working immediately after the VPN disconnects, check VPN lockdown before changing APN settings.

Managed phones and work profiles are different

Android Enterprise allows administrators to configure VPN behavior and restrict whether users can change VPN system settings. Work-profile VPNs can also apply only to managed work apps. If this is a company-managed phone, do not disable the policy on your own; contact the administrator.

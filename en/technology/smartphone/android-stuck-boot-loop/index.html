Use the lowest-impact sequence first

Complete one step at a time and test after each change. Do not change several settings at once.

  1. 1
    Remove the case and cables, confirm no button is stuck, and charge with a known-good charger for about 30 minutes.
  2. 2
    Use the manufacturer’s forced restart procedure once and record any change in the boot sequence.
  3. 3
    If the phone partially starts, use the official model procedure for Safe mode and review recently installed apps.
  4. 4
    Recovery-menu access differs by model; use only official instructions for the exact device.
  5. 5
    Use a non-erasing cache or repair option only when the manufacturer documents it. Do not select Wipe data or Factory reset.
  6. 6
    If the phone cannot start and an erase appears necessary, verify backups, Google Account access, two-step authentication, and eSIM replacement before contacting support.

Do not erase the evidence before isolating the cause

  • Following random button or firmware videos for a different model
  • Selecting Factory reset without understanding the menu
  • Continuing to charge a hot, swollen, or wet phone

Prevent the issue from returning

  • Verify backup status before and after app or OS changes
  • Avoid cases that press or trap the side buttons
If the problem remains

Record the conditions that reproduce the issue: phone model, OS version, time, exact error, recent changes, and steps already tried. This gives the manufacturer, carrier, or app developer useful diagnostic information.

Frequently asked questions

What if my screen looks different?

Menu names and locations vary by OS, manufacturer, model, region, and software version. Use the Settings search field and check the manufacturer guide for your exact model.

When should I stop troubleshooting?

Stop as soon as the issue is fixed. Change one setting at a time so you can identify what solved the problem and reverse it if necessary.

What should I do if the issue remains?

Record how to reproduce it, verify your backup, and contact the manufacturer, carrier, or app provider most closely related to the problem.

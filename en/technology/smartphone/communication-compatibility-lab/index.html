Evaluation rules

  • Separate packet-data success from voice-call success.
  • Separate SMS send/receive from RCS registration and RCS messaging.
  • RCS requires checking OS, carrier/plan, app and the peer endpoint—not just the device.
  • User reports start as pending and never auto-promote to SolQvia real-device evidence.
Important

Band matching or working data alone does not prove VoLTE/IMS, SMS or RCS. RCS also depends on the peer endpoint.

Carrier RCS eligibility is maintained separately from RF/band data. Because conditions change, the checked date and primary URL are retained.

NTT Docomo

As of 2026-08-08, Docomo’s official RCS page lists Android phones/tablets on Android 10+ and says iPhone availability will be announced later. An eligible internet-access service is required; Docomo-network MVNO support is listed as future support.

au / UQ mobile / povo

au officially lists au, UQ mobile, povo and au-network MVNO lines. A line that cannot use SMS cannot use RCS. The published OS floor is Android 8+; iPhone requirements vary by line type between iOS 18.4+ and iOS 26.2+.

Official source ↗

SoftBank / Y!mobile / LINEMO

SoftBank officially lists RCS for SoftBank, Y!mobile and LINEMO. Android requires Android 10+ with Google Messages; iPhone requires iOS 26.4+ with Messages. Business and MVNO lines are listed as future support.

Official source ↗

Rakuten Mobile

Rakuten Mobile documents cases where Android “chat features” affect whether messages are received in Rakuten Link. This ledger does not infer general Rakuten-line RCS eligibility from that FAQ alone.

Official source ↗

iPhone / Apple Messages

Apple states that RCS requires iOS 18+ and a carrier/cellular plan that supports RCS on iPhone. The presence of the toggle alone does not prove successful end-to-end RCS messaging.

Official source ↗

Android / Google Messages

Google Messages exposes RCS states such as connected, connecting, unable to connect and unsupported. SMS/MMS fallback behavior is configured separately when RCS delivery fails.

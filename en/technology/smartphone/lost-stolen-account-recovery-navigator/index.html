Do not enter secrets or device identifiers

Do not enter passwords, SMS/2FA or recovery codes, phone numbers, IMEI/EID/ICCID, eSIM QR codes or activation data. This navigator does not automatically suspend, lock or erase anything.

Priority order

Enter device, line and authentication status to prioritize tracking, lock, line suspension, account protection, 2FA recovery and erase.
Do not retrieve a stolen device yourself

If the location is unfamiliar, contact local law enforcement. Be alert for phishing through messages or fake support contacts.

Keep irreversible actions later

  1. Locate and mark as lost / lock the device
  2. Suspend the SIM/eSIM/mobile line
  3. Review Apple/Google account and trusted devices
  4. Secure non-SMS 2FA and recovery methods
  5. Review wallet, transit, payment and critical services separately
  6. Restore authentication on a replacement SIM/eSIM and device
  7. Remote erase only after deciding recovery is unlikely

On Apple devices, do not remove a stolen device from Find My because that removes Activation Lock. On Android, erase ends further location tracking in Find Hub, so consider location, lock and account recovery first.

How official guidance is separated

Apple/Google device security and carrier line suspension are separate layers. Suspending the line does not necessarily protect all on-device data or every payment service.

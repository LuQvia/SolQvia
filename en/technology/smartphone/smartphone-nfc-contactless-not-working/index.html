Diagnostic order for NFC or Contactless Payment Not Working on a Phone: Checklist

Use the lowest-impact sequence first

Complete one step at a time and test after each change. Do not change several settings at once.

  1. 1
    On Android enable NFC and contactless payments and select the default wallet. On iPhone check the card status in Wallet.
  2. 2
    Unlock and authenticate the phone, then hold the correct phone area steadily against the terminal symbol for several seconds.
  3. 3
    Remove thick cases, magnets, metal rings, and stored contactless cards before testing again.
  4. 4
    Try another compatible terminal or location to separate a terminal problem from a phone problem.
  5. 5
    Before deleting a card, confirm re-enrollment and identity requirements, then update the wallet, OS, and card information.
  6. 6
    If several cards fail at several terminals, report the exact time and error to the payment service, card issuer, and manufacturer.

Do not erase the evidence before isolating the cause

  • Repeating payment attempts without checking for duplicate authorization
  • Deleting cards before confirming how to add them again
  • Testing with another person’s card or account

Prevent the issue from returning

  • Carry a physical card or alternative payment method
  • Review wallet transfer steps before changing phones
If the problem remains

Record the conditions that reproduce the issue: phone model, OS version, time, exact error, recent changes, and steps already tried. This gives the manufacturer, carrier, or app developer useful diagnostic information.

Frequently asked questions

What if my screen looks different?

Menu names and locations vary by OS, manufacturer, model, region, and software version. Use the Settings search field and check the manufacturer guide for your exact model.

When should I stop troubleshooting?

Stop as soon as the issue is fixed. Change one setting at a time so you can identify what solved the problem and reverse it if necessary.

What should I do if the issue remains?

Record how to reproduce it, verify your backup, and contact the manufacturer, carrier, or app provider most closely related to the problem.

Answer first: determine which sound category fails before changing settings

No-sound problems can come from mute and volume settings, Bluetooth or headphone routing, app-specific audio, the earpiece/speaker, or system issues. Test ringtone, media, and call audio separately.

Separate likely causes by symptom

SymptomCheckInterpretation
Only ringtone is missingMute/focus/ringtone volumeNotification and ringtone settings
Only media is missingMedia volume/app/output routeCheck Bluetooth
Only call audio is missingEarpiece/call volume/output routeCompare speakerphone
Bluetooth is connectedReturn output to phone speakerMay not be a hardware fault
All sound is missingRestart/OS/speaker checksHardware becomes more likely

Check one low-impact condition at a time

  1. 1
    Check mute, ringtone volume, and media volume separately
  2. 2
    Check Bluetooth, wired accessories, car audio, and output routing
  3. 3
    Compare ringtone, media playback, and call audio
  4. 4
    Inspect speaker/earpiece openings and case interference
  5. 5
    Restart and check app/OS updates
  6. 6
    If all audio paths remain silent, move to repair

Avoid changing several conditions at once; retest after each change so the cause remains identifiable.

When to move to repair or official support

  • The issue began after a drop, pressure, or liquid exposure
  • It persists after restart, updates, and accessory/environment checks
  • There is overheating, odor, port damage, battery swelling, or another safety concern

Treat erase/factory reset as a later option only after backups and account recovery paths are confirmed.

Separate “a repair channel exists” from “this exact model is accepted today”

Apple

Apple documents iPhone repair, battery service and genuine-part certified repair. Actual eligibility still varies with vintage/obsolete status and parts availability.

Google Pixel

Google documents in/out-of-warranty Pixel repair, and its Japan partner information lists a mail-in route for Pixel 6 and later. Older generations are not auto-promoted into that route.

Google source →

Samsung Galaxy

Samsung documents walk-in/mail-in Galaxy repair using genuine parts. Carrier variants still require an exact-model and original-carrier status check.

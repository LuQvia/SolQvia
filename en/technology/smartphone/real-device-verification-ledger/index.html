Evidence levels

Five distinct evidence states

Real device + official

A first-party observation exists for specific functions and primary sources are also registered.

Official specs + assessment

Primary specifications are reviewed and technically assessed; this is not a device test or carrier guarantee.

Official + assessment

Official checks and specifications support a bounded technical assessment.

Official review

Facts supported by primary sources only; not presented as a real-device test.

Unverified

Evidence is insufficient; the site should not turn this into a definitive compatibility claim.

KY-41C real-device evidence stops at eSIM recognition

The current first-party observation confirms Y!mobile and SoftBank eSIM profile setup and device-side SIM information recognition only. It does not establish APN configuration, packet data, voice/VoLTE/IMS, SMS or monitoring-service operation.

No device-identifying secrets in the public ledger

IMEI, EID, ICCID, phone numbers and subscriber information are not stored in this public dataset. Future user reports will remain separate from SolQvia first-party observations.

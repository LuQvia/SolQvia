Check order used by the diagnosis

  1. Clear Activation Lock / Android device protection and confirm setup with your own account
  2. Check carrier network restriction on the original carrier official site
  3. Check repair/part history and biometric functions
  4. Check battery diagnostics, maximum capacity and cycle count where supported
  5. Test SIM/eSIM, calls, data, SMS, charging and display on-device

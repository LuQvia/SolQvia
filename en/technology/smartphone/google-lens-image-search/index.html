How to search with Google Lens on Android, iPhone, Chrome, and saved photos

Google Lens can search the real world through the camera, saved photos, images on websites, and—on supported Android phones—content already visible on screen. The best entry point depends on what you are trying to search.

Answer first: choose the Lens entry point for the job

TaskUseful entry point
Search something in front of youGoogle app or Chrome Lens camera
Search a saved photoGoogle app, Chrome, Google Photos; iPhone/iPad also supports a saved-image flow through Safari
Search an image on a websiteTouch and hold the image in Google app or Chrome
Search current Android screen contentCircle to Search on supported devices
Copy or translate text in an imageLens text / translate actions

Android: use the Google app or Chrome for camera and saved-image search

  1. Open the Google app or Chrome.
  2. Tap the Google Lens icon in the search bar.
  3. Take a photo or upload an existing image.
  4. Drag the selection box around the part you actually want to search.
  5. Refine the result with an additional question or keyword where the interface offers it.

Google Photos also provides a Lens entry point for saved photos.

iPhone/iPad: Google app, Chrome, and saved-image search

Google currently documents saved-image search on iPhone/iPad through the Google app, Chrome, and Safari. For camera and voice-based Lens search, Google directs users to the Google app.

If saved photos are unavailable, check iOS Settings → Apps → Google → Photos and review whether access is None, Limited, or Full.

Search an image already on a website

In the Google app or Chrome, touch and hold an image and choose the Lens/image-search action. In Chrome, Google states that Google must be the default search engine for website-image Lens search.

If the page contains several objects, narrow the selection to the object, logo, label, or model number that matters.

Supported Android devices can search current screen content with Circle to Search

Circle to Search lets supported Android devices search text or images on the current screen without switching apps. Google documents a long press on the Home button or navigation handle, then circling, highlighting, scribbling, or tapping the target.

Availability depends on device support and settings. Google also lists the Google app version, default digital assistant, and permission to use screen/app data among troubleshooting checks. Some confidential apps may block the feature.

Improve results by narrowing the image region

Google recommends selecting a smaller part of the image for more specific results. Use a tight selection around the product, label, component, plant feature, or text you need.

  • Remove unrelated background from the selection.
  • Move closer when text or markings are small.
  • Add a model number, logo name, or keyword when available.
  • Do not treat the first visual match as definitive identification.
  • Confirm purchase, safety, medical, or authenticity decisions with primary sources.

Visual Search History is separate and off by default

Google states that visual-search technology processes the image used for a search. When the optional Visual Search History setting is enabled while signed in, eligible Google services can save search images to Web & App Activity.

Google states that Visual Search History is off by default. This does not make every image appropriate to upload; crop out unnecessary faces, addresses, documents, account information, or other private details before searching.

Transparency

Publisher, evidence and advertising transparency

This page explains who is responsible for SolQvia.com, how evidence levels are separated, where AI assists, and how editorial decisions remain separate from monetization.

Where readers can start

Start from an exact-model passport, evidence ledger, SIM/eSIM diagnosis, or migration safety check depending on what you already know about the device and problem.

  • Integrated Device Passport
  • Device Verification & Evidence Ledger
  • SIM/eSIM compatibility diagnosis
  • Migration and reset safety diagnosis

Publication purpose

SolQvia.com is a smartphone-focused troubleshooting and technical-verification publication. It separates device model, sales variant, OS, carrier and SIM conditions so readers can reach checks that match their actual configuration.

Publisher and editorial responsibility

The publication is SolQvia.com. Final editorial review is performed by the SolQvia Editorial Lead. Contact: info@solqvia.com. We do not invent a personal name, corporate identity, or brand etymology where no verified first-party source is available.

Evidence levels stay separate

SolQvia keeps first-party device testing, manufacturer/carrier primary sources, technical assessment, and unreviewed user reports as separate evidence layers. A specification or successful eSIM profile installation does not automatically become proof of calls, SMS, RCS or packet data.

Where AI assists

AI may assist topic triage, source organization, outlines, drafts, translation, link checks, structured data and consistency checks. A human reviews destructive actions, account impact, exact-model claims, current official conditions, publication, corrections and updates.

Editorial decisions and monetization

SolQvia completes troubleshooting and technical verification before any comparison route. Where a reader has a genuine comparison or selection need, a relevant route to Luqevora may be shown. Technical conclusions are not changed solely because an advertiser or affiliate program exists. AdSense serving stays disabled until the site is reported Ready.

Privacy and Google services

The Privacy Policy and Cookie Policy disclose the use of Google Analytics, Google Tag Manager, the AdSense base code and a Google-certified CMP. They also explain that third parties such as Google may use cookies or related storage, web beacons, IP addresses or other identifiers in connection with advertising and measurement.

Index-quality control

Audits are run on the completed HTML artifact before index decisions are finalized. As of 9 August 2026, 207 highly similar English carrier-compatibility pages remain publicly accessible but are held at noindex,follow and excluded from the sitemap while individual differentiation continues. This is an internal SolQvia quality-control rule, not a Google or AdSense score.

Pre-review technical gate

  • Root ads.txt matches the AdSense publisher ID.
  • robots.txt does not block the AdSense crawler.
  • The sitemap contains only indexable URLs.
  • Privacy, cookie, advertising, contact and editorial pages remain reachable from the site footer.
  • Auto ads and manual ads remain disabled before approval.
  • SSL, HTTP-to-HTTPS behavior and the AdSense Sites status require a final live check after deployment.

Editorial lead and review process

The SolQvia Editorial Lead performs the final review of procedure safety, device scope, and potential user impact.

Where AI is used

AI assists topic triage, official-source organization, outlines, drafts, translation, consistency, links, and structured-data checks.

What requires human review

  • Potential data loss or account lockout
  • Whether lower-impact actions appear first
  • Whether OS and device differences are overstated
  • Whether escalation conditions are clear
  • Final publication, correction, and updates

How device testing is represented

Guides do not imply hands-on device testing when it was not performed. Concept diagrams are labeled as non-device screenshots.

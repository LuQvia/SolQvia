Transparency / Phase111

Content Quality Audit & Indexing Policy

SolQvia audits whether a page helps a reader distinguish the exact model, carrier and conditions needed to reach an answer, rather than treating page count as a quality goal.

What Phase111 audits

  • Title, description, canonical, hreflang and structured-data consistency
  • Template similarity across sales-variant pages and the amount of page-specific analysis
  • Primary sources, check dates and the separation of first-party testing, technical assessment and unverified items
  • Intentional noindex for utilities such as internal search and 404 pages
  • Purpose, content depth and navigation across Japanese and English pages

Automation is not used to justify page volume

SolQvia uses structured data, scripts and AI to assist organization, drafting, translation and consistency checks. The quality policy does not treat model-name substitution at scale as sufficient value. Device pages preserve the factors that can change an outcome: sales channel, exact model record, source frequency table, SIM/eSIM, VoLTE/IMS and open evidence gaps.

What Phase111 changes

In the Phase110 baseline, English Japan-carrier sales-variant pages had a concentration of duplicate titles and descriptions. Phase111 adds the exact sales channel to titles and H1s and adds page-specific comparisons of the bands listed in each primary-source record versus sibling Japan sales records.

Important: A band missing from one carrier frequency table is not automatically treated as proof that the radio hardware lacks the band. Source-table scope, global hardware specifications, VoLTE/IMS, APN, eSIM provisioning and carrier certification are separate layers.

About the A/B/C quality classes

A/B/C is an internal SolQvia self-audit, not a Google or AdSense score. Automated checks cover metadata uniqueness, canonical integrity, structured data, evidence visibility, page-specific information and duplication risk. B/C pages remain in a review queue.

Who, how and why

  • Who: The SolQvia Editorial Lead performs final publication, correction and update review.
  • How: Primary sources and structured records are preferred. AI/automation assists organization, drafting, translation and QA. A page never implies hands-on testing where none occurred.
  • Why: The site exists to answer condition-specific questions such as what happens with this exact model, carrier and configuration.

Phase112 / B-queue re-audit

Recompute the finished artifact and hold highly templated English pages from indexing

Phase111 had an internal sequencing mismatch: when the finished release artifact is recomputed with the same character n-gram TF-IDF method, the number of English carrier-compatibility pages at or above 0.98 is 233, not the stored intermediate value of 83. Phase112 fixes the audit order so similarity is computed from the final HTML.

The 10 Japanese B-queue pages now expose official primary sources, evidence boundaries and safe verification order in the main content. English sales-record pages received compact record-specific band sets, sibling-variant deltas, carrier notes and exact-record sources, reducing the 0.98+ count to 207.

Phase125: selective reindex after search-signal and uniqueness review

From the pages held in Phase124, only 36 pages were returned to the index after rechecking search demand together with page-specific depth, primary-source support and template similarity. High-similarity or thin pages remain available but noindex,follow.

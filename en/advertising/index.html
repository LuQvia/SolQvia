Advertising & Affiliate Policy

We separate editorial judgment from advertising relationships.

Comparison and selection links from SolQvia to Luqevora

SolQvia prioritizes problem solving, diagnosis, and technical verification, with display advertising as its primary monetization model. When a reader has finished troubleshooting and needs to compare or select a service, SolQvia may link to the comparison media site Luqevora only when the destination is directly relevant.

Pages on Luqevora may contain advertising or affiliate links. Simply visiting Luqevora from SolQvia does not create a charge, and SolQvia does not insert comparison links where they are not relevant to the problem being solved.

Pre-advertising status

Ad slots remain hidden until the correct AdSense publisher ID, site-review setting, and certified CMP are configured. Placeholder publisher IDs are never published.

Editorial independence

Topics, evaluations, and conclusions are not determined solely by advertiser or commission relationships. Advertising and affiliate relationships are disclosed clearly.

Ad placement

Phase98 uses controlled placements: standard articles may use semantic breaks and the article end, sensitive-operation articles are limited to the article end, and interactive diagnosis, recovery, error-lookup, and model-search tools remain ad-free.

Ads are clearly labeled and kept away from navigation, buttons, forms, download actions, and Luqevora referral CTAs where accidental clicks could occur. Space is reserved before rendering to reduce layout shift.

Affiliate links

Commission links are labeled as advertising or sponsored content and use the sponsored link attribute. Confirm final prices and terms with the official provider.

Cookies and consent

A Google-certified CMP is used where required for Google advertising. See the Cookie Policy.

Measuring SolQvia-to-Luqevora revenue assistance

When a visitor moves from SolQvia to Luqevora, a non-personal bridge ID may be carried forward so the source article and comparison route can be associated with later advertising interactions during that visit.

For A8.net advertising links on Luqevora, SolQvia-origin sessions may append an alphanumeric bridge token to the A8 parameter id5. The token does not contain personal information or device-specific identifiers.
